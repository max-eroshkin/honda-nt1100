# Front Suspension Specifications

FRONT WHEEL/SUSPENSION/STEERING SPECIFICATIONS

**Cold tire pressure:**
* Driver only - 250 kPa (2.5 kgf/cm³, 36 psi)
* Driver and passenger - 250 kPa (2.5 kgf/cm³, 36 psi)

**Axle runout** - 0.2 (0.01)

**Wheel rim runout:**, mm (in)
* Radial - 2.0 (0.08)
* Axial – 2.0 (0.08)

**Wheel balance weight** – 60 g (2.1 oz) max.

**Fork:**
* Spring free length, mm (in)
  * **R** - 395.1 (15.56) R:387.2 (15.24)
  * **L** - 469.8 (18.50) L:460.4 (18.13)
* Recommended fork fluid - Fork Fluid (viscosity: 10W)
* Fluid level, mm (in)
  * **R** - 72 (2.8)
  * **L** - 196 (7.7)
* Fluid capacity
  * **R** - 600 ± 2.5 cm³ (20.3 ± 0.1 US oz, 21.1 ±0.1 Imp oz)
  * **L** - 593 ± 2.5 cm³ (20.1 ± 0.1 US oz, 20.9 ±0.1 Imp oz)
* Pre-load adjuster standard position
  * MT model - 7 turns from the full soft position
  * DCT model - 10 turns from the full soft position
