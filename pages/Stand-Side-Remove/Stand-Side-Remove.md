# Stand-Side Remove

REMOVAL

Remove the sidestand
switch bolt [1] and
sidestand switch [2].

![Image page-001-img-01.jpg](images/page-001-img-01.jpg)

Support the motorcycle
securely using a Mainstand, hoist or equivalent.  
Remove the sidestand return spring [1].  
Remove the sidestand pivot nut [2] and bolt
[3], then remove the sidestand [4] and collar [5].
