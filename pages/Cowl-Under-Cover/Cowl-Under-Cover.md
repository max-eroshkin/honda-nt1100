# Cowl - Under Cover

REMOVAL/INSTALLATION

Remove the following:
* Socket bolt A [1]
* Socket bolts B [2]
* Socket bolt C [3]
* Washer A [4]
* Washers B [5]
* Grommet A [6]
* Grommet B [7]
* Collar A [8]
* Collars B [9]
* Collar C [10]

Release the boss [11] from the grommet [12].

Remove the under cover [13].

Installation is in the reverse order of removal.
